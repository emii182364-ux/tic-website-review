# Website Review: taipeichurch.org

Errors, inconsistencies, features and recommendations  ·  Reviewed September 27, 2026

## Summary

We reviewed all 17 public pages of taipeichurch.org and checked 73 unique links. The site is live and most links work, but visitors can currently run into template placeholder content, a broken calendar, conflicting service times, and Zoom links that go through old newsletter tracking links. ## 5. What the "Log In" button does

The "Log In" button in the top menu belongs to the Wix Members Area app. It lets a visitor sign up with an email address (or Google/Facebook) and log in to a personal account with these pages:

- **My Account:** name, photo and contact details.
- **Profile and Followers:** a public member profile that other members can follow.
- **Notifications and Settings:** email and site notification preferences.
Members Area is normally used to unlock members-only pages, blog comments, forum posts, event tickets or online payments. On taipeichurch.org none of these is in use: there are no members-only pages, the Blog page (/blog) is published but has no posts and is not in the menu, and the Forum is installed but not published. As a result, the button currently gives visitors nothing except an account they do not need, and the church collects sign-ups it may not be managing.

**Recommendation:** either hide the Log In button (Wix Editor → select the login bar → hide, or remove the Members Area app), or give it a clear purpose, for example a members-only page with the church directory, serving schedules, or small group materials.

### Other installed apps to review

The site loads several Wix apps that are unused or duplicated. Each one adds loading time on every page:

- **Two visitor analytics tools** (TWIPLA Website Intelligence and Web-Stat). Keep one, or use Wix's built-in analytics.
- **Legacy and duplicate form apps** (Old Wix Forms and Payments, 123 Form Builder, Forms & Payments).
- **Unused apps:** Wix Blog (no posts), Wix Forum, Wix Chat, Wix Music, Countdown Timer, ShoutOut (Legacy), Instagram Feed and Social Media Stream.
## 6. Recommendations

### Quick wins

- **Put key information in text, not images.** The service time, online time and address are mainly shown inside banner images, which search engines and screen readers cannot read.
- **Add SEO descriptions.** 16 of 17 pages have no meta description. Page titles end in "| taipeichurch"; "| Taipei International Church" reads better in search results.
- **Add alt text to images.** 47 images have none (16 of 25 on the Serve page alone).
- **Add the Chinese address to the homepage** (台北市中山區明水路397巷7弄41號B1) so visitors can show it to a taxi driver. The Groups page already does this for the Filipino Ministry.
### Language: English first, with a Traditional Chinese option

As an international church, the site should always open in English. Traditional Chinese (繁體中文) should be available as an option for local friends and family invited by members.

Wix Multilingual is already turned on for taipeichurch.org, but English is the only language set up. Adding Chinese does not require a new website:

1. In the Wix dashboard, go to Settings → Language & Region → Multilingual.
1. Keep English as the main language, so every visitor sees English by default.
1. Add Chinese (Traditional, Taiwan) as a secondary language.
1. Translate the pages with Wix's machine translation, then have a native speaker review the key pages: Home, Plan Your Visit, Families, Groups and Giving.
1. Show the language menu in the header as "EN | 中文". Turn off automatic redirection by browser language, so English stays the default for everyone.
1. Keep the Chinese address (台北市中山區明水路397巷7弄41號B1) visible on the English site as well.
### Bigger improvements

- **Create a "Plan Your Visit" page.** Reuse the existing /visit page: what happens on a first Sunday, kids check-in, how to get there from Dazhi MRT, where to find the welcome table.
- **Keep one source for schedules.** Connect a single Google Calendar and use it on Groups, Families and What's Happening, so times stop conflicting.
- **Simplify the menu.** The menu currently has 17 items. Group them under Visit, About, Connect, Messages and Give.
- **Improve mobile speed.** The homepage HTML alone is about 1.1 MB, so it loads slowly on phones. Compress images and remove unused sections.
