## About this inventory

This inventory records how every page of the current website is built: each element in order, the exact Wix animation it uses (effect, direction, duration, delay and what starts it), and every gallery, carousel, slideshow, video and hover effect. It was read from the data the live site loads, not guessed. Use it to rebuild the site the same way (or better) in a new Wix site or another platform.

**How to read the animation column:** "FloatIn, direction right, 1200ms" means the element floats in from the right over 1.2 seconds. "on scroll into view" means it starts when the visitor scrolls to it; it plays once per page view.
