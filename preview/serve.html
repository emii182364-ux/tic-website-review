    SERVE

    Worship Team

    We work together to bring about an atmosphere of meaningful worship.

    Welcome Team

     We greet people and welcome them to the home of God.

    Tech Team

    We run the sound and video for Sunday worship service. 

    Media Team

    We capture moments of Sunday services and share via YouTube live streams to the people who cannot be there in person.

    Kids Ministry

    We lead the Sunday program for children, preschool through grade 5 in order to help them become and grow as disciples of Christ. 

    Hospitality Team

    We care for the church and for those who are in need.

    Youth Ministry

    We engage with young people who attend their places of worship, or who live in their community.

    If you would like to learn more, talk to one of our volunteers at welcome table.
