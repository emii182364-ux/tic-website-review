Your first Sunday

New to TIC? Here is what to expect, from the moment you arrive until you say hello.

  1. 1
    Step 1

    Arrive a little before 10:30

    Look for the blue TIC sign and head down to B1.

  2. 2
    Step 2

    Worship together

    Families stay together for the singing, then children from Pre-K to grade 12 go to their programs.

  3. 3
    Step 3

    Check in your kids

    A parent or authorized adult checks each child in and out. Nursery care is available during the service.

  4. 4
    Step 4

    Say hello at the welcome table

    Ask questions, sign up for the newsletter or find a way to serve.
