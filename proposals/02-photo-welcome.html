What to expect

Your first Sunday

Visiting for the first time? Here's how a Sunday morning at TIC works.

  1. 01

    Arrive a little before 10:30

    Look for the blue TIC sign and head down to B1.

  2. 02

    Worship together

    Families stay together for the singing, then children from Pre-K to grade 12 go to their programs.

  3. 03

    Check in your kids

    A parent or authorized adult checks each child in and out. Nursery care during the service.

  4. 04

    Say hello at the welcome table

    Ask questions, sign up for the newsletter or find a way to serve.
