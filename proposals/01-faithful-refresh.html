Your firstSunday

What to expect on your first visit, from the moment you arrive to the welcome table.

  1. 01

    Arrive a little before 10:30

    Look for the blue TIC sign and head down to B1.

  2. 02

    Worship together

    Families stay together for the singing, then children from Pre-K to grade 12 go to their programs.

  3. 03

    Check in your kids

    A parent or authorized adult checks each child in and out. Nursery care during the service.

  4. 04

    Say hello at the welcome table

    Ask questions, sign up for the newsletter or find a way to serve.

Aboutthe church

Who we are

TIC is a non-denominational, English-speaking church, welcoming people from every nation and background since 1957.

Our vision

Disciples who shine the light of God's love on Taipei, Taiwan and the world.

Our values

  1. 01

    Bible based

    Scripture shapes what we believe and how we live.

  2. 02

    Valuing diversity

    Many nations, cultures and ages, united in Christ.

  3. 03

    Expecting transformation

    “Come as you are, but don't stay as you came.”

  4. 04

    Building community

    Life happens in small groups and serving teams.

  5. 05

    Practicing servanthood

    Following Jesus by serving others.
