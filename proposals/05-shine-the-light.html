Your first Sunday

Here is what to expect when you join us for the first time.

  1. 01

    Arrive a little before 10:30

    Look for the blue TIC sign and head down to B1.

  2. 02

    Worship together

    Families stay together for the singing, then children from Pre-K to grade 12 go to their programs.

  3. 03

    Check in your kids

    A parent or authorized adult checks each child in and out. Nursery care during the service.

  4. 04

    Say hello at the welcome table

    Ask questions, sign up for the newsletter or find a way to serve.

Who we are

About the church

Taipei International Church is a non-denominational, English-speaking church, welcoming people from every nation and background since 1957.

A multicultural, multigenerational church filled with the power of the Holy Spirit, proclaiming the good news in word and actions.
