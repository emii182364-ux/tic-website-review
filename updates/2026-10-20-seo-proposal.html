Google search proposal

20 October 2026 · Prepared by the Páginas Clover team for the TIC website team

In short

Goal: help people who search for an English-speaking church in Taipei find TIC, and make TIC's results on Google clear and inviting.

Most urgent: Google is still sending visitors to the old "New pastor" page, which now shows "Page not found". We will send them to Meet the Team instead.

What changes: only how the website appears on Google (titles, short descriptions, church details and old addresses). Visitors will not see any difference on the pages themselves.

What we ask: your OK to apply it this week. Nothing is deleted, and everything can be changed back.

Problems we see in the search data

Search numbers: Wix dashboard, Google results, 19 September – 16 October 2026 (28 days). Page checks: live site, 19–20 October 2026.

Urgent

Google sends people to a page that no longer exists

The old "New pastor" page (/newpastor) now shows "Page not found", but Google still lists it: it appeared 50 times and 12 people clicked and landed on an error page. The same can happen with older addresses that were removed (old testimonies, /visit, /oldserve, /blog).

People only find TIC when they already know its name

"taipei international church" was shown 229 times (position 1.8). The general search "international church taipei" was shown only 20 times, at position 7.9, near the bottom of the first page. Other English searches people use to look for a church, like "English church Taipei", don't appear in the dashboard at all.

Many people see TIC on Google but don't click

The homepage was shown 2,640 times and clicked 171 times (6%). The Groups page was shown 617 times and clicked 9 times (1.5%). Their titles ("Home | taipeichurch", "Groups | taipeichurch") and missing descriptions don't tell people what they will find.

Google receives three different addresses

The pages show No. 41, Aly. 7, Ln. 397, Mingshui Rd. The hidden information Google reads still says "575 Mingshui Road, 10491", and the homepage map button goes to "569 Mingshui Rd, B2". Conflicting addresses make Google and maps less confident about where the church is.

Google sees TIC as a generic business called "taipeichurch"

The hidden data describes a "LocalBusiness" named "taipeichurch" instead of a church named "Taipei International Church".

Old, sample and duplicate pages are listed for Google

Two Wix sample events with a San Francisco address, a calendar that was never connected, an empty Livestream page, a 2019 discussion guide, six event pages all titled "Registration Form", a page called "Copy of Serve", and 12 addresses that start with "copy-of".

Pages and photos are hard for Google to understand

40 of 41 pages have no description, only 6 of 35 pages have a main heading, and 169 of 398 photos have no description or only a file name like "IMG_0962.JPG", so they can't show up in Google Images.

No Chinese at all

There is no Chinese version and no Chinese address on the homepage, so people who search in Chinese (for example 台北 英文 教會, "English church Taipei") have nothing to find.

What we will do

  1. Site name. Change it from "taipeichurch" to "Taipei International Church". Every page title will end with the church's real name.
  2. Titles and descriptions for the 17 main pages (table below). Written from the real content of each page, using the words people search for, and short enough that Google shows them in full.
  3. Church details for Google. Name, the new address (B1, No. 41, Aly. 7, Ln. 397, Mingshui Rd., Zhongshan Dist., Taipei City 10466), email and Sunday 10:30 AM service, so Google stops showing the old address (575 Mingshui Road).
  4. Hide old or empty pages from Google (they stay on the site and can be shown again anytime):
