Taipei International Church · taipeichurch.org

Website status report

19 October 2026 · Prepared by the Páginas Clover team for the TIC website team

In short

Thank you for giving our team access to the TIC website. We checked every page of the live site and the Google numbers in the Wix dashboard.

Most people find TIC by searching its exact name. Searches like "international church Taipei" bring very few visitors, and the Groups page appears in Google often but is almost never clicked.

Most problems from our September review are still on the site (it was republished on 15 October). The most urgent ones are wrong or conflicting information: service times, sample events from a Wix template, and an old address that Google still reads.

All of it can be fixed inside Wix, in small steps, and we will show you each change before it goes live.

3. What we would like to improve

Proposals

Next steps after the fixes

  • A "Plan your visit" page for first-time guests: time, how to get there from Dazhi MRT (with your walk video), what to expect, and kids check-in.MNew page
  • A clearer Groups page that answers "when, where, who it's for" at a glance, so more of the people who see it on Google click through.MGroups
  • A short homepage with clear links to one page per topic (Families, Groups, Serve, Give). We prepared a design proposal in this style that you can review.LHome and main pages
  • A Traditional Chinese version, or at least the address and service times in Chinese on the homepage, for Chinese-speaking friends and family.LSite
  • Make TIC easier to recommend for AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity): clear answers to common questions on the site, consistent address and times everywhere.MSite
  • A practical Contact page: phone, office hours, address with map, and a few common questions.MContact

One limit to know: some technical security settings (security headers) can't be changed on Wix, so a few external security scans will keep showing a lower grade. This does not affect visitors.

How we will work

  1. Before any change, we make sure Wix's version history has a saved copy we can go back to.
  2. We fix things in small groups, starting with Priority 1.
  3. We send you a short note with what changed and screenshots before publishing.
  4. Nothing is deleted (pages, apps, events) without your OK.
